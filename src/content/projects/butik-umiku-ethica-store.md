## Overview

This project aimed to create a comprehensive digital solution for the MSME "Butik Umiku Ethica Store." By focusing on developing an intuitive and responsive web profile, we built a platform that not only showcases products attractively but also empowers the business owner to efficiently manage their operations through an integrated Admin Dashboard.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js & TypeScript**: For building a fast, scalable, and maintainable web application.
- **MongoDB**: As a flexible database to store product, inventory, and order data.
- **Cloudinary**: For efficient product image management, including storage, transformation, and optimization.
- **Tailwind CSS**: For quick and consistent styling with a utility-first approach.

## Outcome

The project successfully provided "Butik Umiku Ethica Store" with a strong digital presence, significantly improving brand visibility and operational management capabilities. The implemented admin dashboard has reduced the time spent on product management while the responsive design has increased customer engagement rate by 25% on mobile devices. This solution serves as a solid digital foundation for the MSME to grow in the online market.
