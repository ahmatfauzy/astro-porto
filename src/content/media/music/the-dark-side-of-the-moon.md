Released in March 1973. The album draws from everyday experiences — time, money, travel, and death — with a prism cover created by Hipgnosis and George Hardie.

## Pushing the Everyday into the Cosmos

Clocks, cash registers, and breathing are not decoration; they are woven into the skeleton of the melody. Pink Floyd stretches the most ordinary anxieties — running late, talking money, fearing loss of control — into a sound map wide enough to hold them.

So the album does not need to be read as a grand conceptual work to stand. With headphones on, the urgency inside "Time" still feels as if it grew out of your own life.
